# Sources and attribution

This repository distributes a derived HSK vocabulary dataset. The licences
below apply to the data files named here.

## HSK word list

- Source: `complete-hsk-vocabulary` by Yanis Zafirópulos
  <https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary>
- Additional source: official HSK 3.0 vocabulary levels
- Licence: MIT
- Changes: normalised to simplified Chinese; mapped to HSK 3.0 levels;
  part-of-speech tags normalised.

## Definitions

- Source: CC-CEDICT
  <https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict>
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- Changes: converted from CC-CEDICT line format; senses joined with `; `;
  pinyin and part-of-speech tags normalised; English definitions translated
  and editorially adapted into Korean.

The Korean definitions are adaptations of the CC-CEDICT definitions and are
released under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Suggested attribution

> HSK word list derived from complete-hsk-vocabulary (MIT) and the official
> HSK 3.0 lists. Definitions adapted from CC-CEDICT (CC BY-SA 4.0), with
> Korean translations by Junggugeo.
